# STC Customer Experience Analysis (NLP & Power BI) 📊
A comprehensive project analyzing stc customer sentiments on X (Twitter) using Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Interactive Dashboards to transform raw text into actionable business insights.

### 🎯 Project Objectives
Understanding Customer Voice: Extracting authentic customer opinions by filtering out automated or official corporate responses.

Local Sentiment Analysis: Utilizing AI models specifically tuned for the Saudi dialect and local context.

### 🛠 Tech Stack
Python: Data cleaning and preprocessing.

AI & NLP: SaudiBERT model for high-accuracy dialect processing.

Visualization: WordCloud for thematic analysis and Matplotlib/Seaborn for statistical charts.

Business Intelligence: Power BI for interactive reporting.

### 📂 Workflow
Advanced Cleaning: Removed official stc accounts (e.g., stccare), handled text normalization (removing tashkeel and decorations), and eliminated duplicates.

Hybrid Sentiment Analysis: Combined the power of SaudiBERT with a custom local keyword dictionary (e.g., "كفو", "خايس", "نصب") to enhance classification accuracy.

Visualization: Generated sentiment-specific Word Clouds and statistical distribution charts.

Reporting: Integrated all insights into a Power BI dashboard for decision-makers.

### 📈 Results & Insights
Sentiment Distribution: Analysis revealed that 71.7% of interactions are neutral inquiries, 21.2% are negative, and 7.1% are positive.

Customer Pain Points (Negative): Key concerns revolved around technical issues and service requests, highlighted by words like "المشكلة" (The Problem), "بلاغ" (Report), and "الغاء" (Cancellation).

Satisfaction Drivers (Positive): High satisfaction was linked to service speed and device availability, with frequent mentions of "افضل" (Best), "سريع" (Fast), and "متوفر" (Available).
